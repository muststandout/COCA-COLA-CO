# COCA-COLA-CO
Excel Power Query, DATA Visualization(Dashboard)
___________________________________________________________________________________________________________
COCA COLA CO financial data converted into a presentable format to quickly showcase gain & losses.
Marketing teams could then use this information to go over company events and trends throughout the time period
to predict a trend of what causes the growth and what causes the deficit.
--COCA COLA CO (Sheet 1) is the raw data grabbed from Kaggle
--Negative_cash_flow (sheet 5) are the years that are doing relatively bad
--Dashboard (sheet 7) is the interactive dashboard made for other members to take a closer look at specific
Year or statements
